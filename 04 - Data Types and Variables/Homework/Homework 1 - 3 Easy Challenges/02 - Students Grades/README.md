**Set:** Variables Homework 1  
**Problem:** 2  
**Source status:** Verified assignment

## Description

Read two students' names, IDs, and math grades, then display their information and the average grade.

## Input

Name, ID, and grade for each of two students.

## Output

Each student's information and the mean of the two grades.

## Requirements and constraints

Grades may contain fractional values; use an appropriate type.

## Example

Grades 20 and 30 have average 25.
