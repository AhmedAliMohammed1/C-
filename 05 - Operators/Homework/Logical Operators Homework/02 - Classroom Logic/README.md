## Description

Prepare and print a boolean for each of the eight classroom conditions listed below.

## Input

Numbers of boys, girls, and teachers.

## Output

One boolean result per condition.

## Requirements and constraints

Translate each stated condition into a logical expression.

## Assignment context

Let nb, ng, nt denote boys, girls, and teachers. Prepare a boolean for each condition:

1. More than 25 boys.
2. At most 30 girls.
3. More than 20 boys and more than 2 teachers, or more than 30 girls and more than 4 teachers.
4. Fewer than 60 boys or fewer than 70 girls.
5. Neither at least 60 boys nor at least 70 girls.
6. Exactly 10 more boys than girls.
7. The difference between the boy and girl counts is more than 10, or there are more than 5 teachers.
8. Exactly 10 more boys than girls, or exactly 15 more girls than boys.

The slide does not further clarify whether “difference” in condition 7 is directional or absolute; retain that ambiguity rather than assuming an unstated rule.
