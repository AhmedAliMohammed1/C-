# 1 - Arithmetic Ratios

**Course:** Mastering 4 Critical SKILLS Using C++ 17  
**Section:** 05 - Operators  
**Set:** Division and Modulus Homework 1  
**Problem:** 1  
**Source status:** Verified assignment

## Description

Compute the mean of all five numbers, the sum of the first three divided by the sum of the last two, and the corresponding ratio of their means.

## Input

Five numbers in order.

## Output

The three results.

## Requirements and constraints

Consider the relationship between the two ratios; use a type that preserves fractional results.

## Example

1 2 3 4 5: mean 3; sum ratio 6/9; mean ratio 2/4.5.
