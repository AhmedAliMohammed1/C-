# 2 - Days to Years Months and Days

**Course:** Mastering 4 Critical SKILLS Using C++ 17  
**Section:** 05 - Operators  
**Set:** Division and Modulus Homework 3  
**Problem:** 2  
**Source status:** Verified assignment

## Description

Convert a total number of days into years, months, and remaining days.

## Input

An integer number of days.

## Output

Year, month, and day counts.

## Requirements and constraints

Assume 12 months per year and 30 days per month, so a year has 360 days.

## Example

391 -> 1 year, 1 month, 1 day.
